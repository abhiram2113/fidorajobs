UNDERSTAND YOUR MATCH

A useful signal.
Not a hiring promise.

The match percentage compares the skills and words in your resume with the ones mentioned in that job. If a role asks for Excel and SQL, the score checks whether those terms are in your resume. It does not tell you your chance of getting hired.

For the clearest comparison, keep your resume accurate and up to date, and read the complete employer posting.

QUICK ANSWERS

Know what to expect

Do I apply for jobs inside FidoraJobs?

No. FidoraJobs helps you discover listings and provides direct links. You review and submit each application on the employer’s official career website.

What does the match percentage mean?

It estimates overlap between skills and keywords in your resume/profile and those found in a job listing. It is not a hiring probability or guarantee. Read the full posting before applying.

Does FidoraJobs include every job from every company?

FidoraJobs brings together opportunities available in its listings. Coverage and freshness can vary, so check an employer’s careers page for the latest openings.
