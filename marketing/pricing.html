A FEW DETAILS

Before you choose

How do I purchase a plan?

Choose a plan to open your UPI app with the fixed amount. After paying, return here and submit the UPI reference number (UTR). We will check the payment manually and contact you on WhatsApp after you send the payment details.

Will FidoraJobs submit applications for me?

You stay in control. FidoraJobs helps you find listings, and you follow each direct link to apply on the employer’s career portal.

Does a resume match guarantee an interview?

Your match score is an estimate of the skills and keywords your resume shares with a role. Use it to decide what to explore, then read the full listing on the employer’s site.
