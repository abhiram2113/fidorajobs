A FEW DETAILS

Before you choose

How do I purchase a plan?

Select a plan to open an email draft to support@fidorapath.in and confirm enrollment and payment instructions. This page does not process payment.

Will FidoraJobs submit applications for me?

No. FidoraJobs helps you discover listings. You follow the direct link and apply on the employer’s career portal.

Does a resume match guarantee an interview?

No. Match scores estimate keyword and skill overlap only. They are not hiring probabilities or promises of selection.
