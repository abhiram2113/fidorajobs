Good to know.

Will I need to pay every month?

No. These plans are presented as one-time payments with no recurring subscription renewal fee.

How much does the Duo plan cost in total?

₹598 total for two members, or ₹299 per member. Compared with two individual plans at ₹499 each, the combined saving is ₹400 (about 40.08%).

How does the three-friend plan work?

The plan is ₹297 total for three members, or ₹99 each. Compared with three individual plans at ₹499 each, the combined saving is ₹1,200 (about 80.16%). Confirm referral eligibility and enrolment details with support.

Does lifetime access guarantee a job?

No. FidoraJobs is a job-search tool, not an employer or recruitment guarantee. Hiring decisions, vacancy status, and outcomes depend on employers.

What if a job link is no longer active?

Job listings can close or change at any time. Always check the employer's official career page for current availability and requirements.

Email support@fidorapath.in with your question and preferred plan.
