A SCORE YOU CAN UNDERSTAND

What does
“86% match”
actually mean?

It’s an estimate of how much the listing’s skills and keywords overlap with the information in your resume and career preferences.

  • ✓ Compare role requirements with your resume keywords
  • ✓ Spot roles that may deserve a closer look
  • ✓ Use the score as a guide for your own decision

A match score is not a hiring probability, a promise of an interview, or a guarantee of selection. Always read the full listing.
