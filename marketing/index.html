A SCORE YOU CAN UNDERSTAND

What does
“86% match”
actually mean?

The score compares skills and words in your resume with the skills and words mentioned in a job listing. For example, if a Data Analyst role asks for Excel and SQL, the score checks whether those words also appear in your resume.

  • ✓ See which skills and words appear in both
  • ✓ Decide which job details you want to read first
  • ✓ Then read the full job description yourself

A match score is not a hiring probability, a promise of an interview, or a guarantee of selection. Always read the full listing.
